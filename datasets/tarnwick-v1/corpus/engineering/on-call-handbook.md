# On-call Handbook

Document owner: Site Reliability Engineering

## Rotation

Each product team runs a weekly on-call rotation with a primary and a secondary engineer. Handover happens every Monday at 11:00 in a short call, where the outgoing engineer walks through open alerts and recent changes.

## Being on call

While on call, keep your company laptop and phone with you and stay within reach of a reliable internet connection. If you cannot be available for part of your shift, arrange a swap with a teammate and update the schedule in the paging tool.

## Handling alerts

Treat every alert as real until proven otherwise. Follow the linked runbook first, and declare an incident as soon as customers may be affected; severity definitions and escalation rules are in the Incident Response Runbook.

## Health and workload

After a night with more than two pages, the on-call engineer starts late the next day. Teams review the number of pages every month and fix noisy alerts as a priority.
