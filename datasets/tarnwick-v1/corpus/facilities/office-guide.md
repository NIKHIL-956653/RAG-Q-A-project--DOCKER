# Office Guide: Tarnwick Head Office

Document owner: Facilities

## Access and badges

The office is open from 07:00 to 20:00 on working days. Employees enter with their personal badge; a lost badge must be reported to the reception desk on the same day so that it can be blocked. Badges are not transferable.

Visitors must be registered by their host in the visitor system at least 24 hours before the visit and must be accompanied at all times.

## Workspaces and meeting rooms

Desks are not assigned and can be booked in the workplace app up to two weeks ahead. Meeting rooms on the third floor are reserved for customer visits on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The quiet room on the second floor is phone-free.

## Lockers and equipment storage

Lockers are available on every floor for personal items. Company laptops must not be left in lockers overnight; take them home or lock them in the IT cabinet next to the service desk.

## Climate and comfort

The heating and ventilation system keeps office temperatures between 20 °C and 23 °C. Report problems with heating, lighting or cleaning through the facilities portal.

## Safety

Fire drills take place twice a year. The assembly point is the car park on the north side of the building. First aid kits are located next to each kitchen.
