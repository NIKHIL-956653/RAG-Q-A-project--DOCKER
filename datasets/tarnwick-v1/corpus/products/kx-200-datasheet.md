# KX-200 Air Quality Sensor: Datasheet

Product family: KX environmental sensors
Datasheet revision: C (October 2025)

## Overview

The KX-200 is a battery-powered indoor and sheltered-outdoor sensor that measures particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), temperature and relative humidity. It reports to the Tarnwick Relay Gateway over LoRaWAN.

## Environmental ratings

- Operating temperature: -20 °C to 60 °C
- Storage temperature: -30 °C to 70 °C
- Ingress protection: IP65 (dust tight, protected against water jets)

## Power

The KX-200 runs on two AA lithium batteries. Typical battery life is 18 months at the default 15-minute sampling interval. Shorter sampling intervals reduce battery life proportionally.

## Measurement

The sampling interval can be configured between 1 and 60 minutes. The particulate sensor has a measurement range of 0 to 1,000 µg/m³.

## Connectivity

- Radio: LoRaWAN, EU868 band
- Typical range: up to 2 km line of sight to the gateway

## Physical

Dimensions: 110 × 80 × 35 mm. Weight including batteries: 190 g. Mounting: wall bracket included.
