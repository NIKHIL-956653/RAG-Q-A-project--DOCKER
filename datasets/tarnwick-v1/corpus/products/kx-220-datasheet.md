# KX-220 Industrial Air Quality Sensor: Datasheet

Product family: KX environmental sensors
Datasheet revision: A (February 2026)

## Overview

The KX-220 is the industrial variant of the KX family. In addition to particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), temperature and relative humidity, it measures carbon dioxide (CO2) and volatile organic compounds (VOC). It reports to the Tarnwick Relay Gateway over LoRaWAN or LTE-M.

## Environmental ratings

- Operating temperature: -40 °C to 70 °C
- Storage temperature: -40 °C to 80 °C
- Ingress protection: IP67 (dust tight, protected against temporary immersion)

## Power

The KX-220 runs on one D-size lithium thionyl chloride battery. Typical battery life is 36 months at the default 15-minute sampling interval. Using LTE-M instead of LoRaWAN roughly halves the battery life.

## Measurement

The sampling interval can be configured between 1 and 120 minutes. The CO2 sensor has a measurement range of 400 to 5,000 ppm.

## Connectivity

- Radio: LoRaWAN (EU868 band) with LTE-M fallback
- Typical range: up to 5 km line of sight to the gateway over LoRaWAN

## Physical

Dimensions: 140 × 95 × 48 mm. Weight including battery: 420 g. Mounting: stainless steel pole clamp included.
