# KX Sensor Installation Guide

Applies to: KX-200 and KX-220 sensors
Guide revision: 4

## Choosing a location

Mount sensors at a height of 1.5 to 2 metres, away from direct sunlight, ventilation outlets and exhaust pipes. Indoors, keep at least 1 metre of distance from doors and windows. Outdoors, the KX-200 must be mounted under a roof or shelter; the KX-220 may be mounted in exposed locations.

## Mounting

The KX-200 is fixed with the wall bracket and two screws. The KX-220 pole clamp fits poles with a diameter of 40 to 60 mm. Tighten the clamp by hand and then a quarter turn with a spanner.

## Inserting batteries

Open the rear cover with the supplied key and insert the batteries in the orientation shown inside the housing. The status LED blinks green while the sensor joins the network.

## Pairing with a gateway

Register the sensor serial number in the customer portal and assign it to a gateway. The sensor joins over LoRaWAN within a few minutes when it is in range. If the LED blinks orange for more than ten minutes, move the sensor closer to the gateway and restart it by removing the batteries for 30 seconds.

## After installation

Check in the customer portal that measurements arrive at the configured interval. Clean the air inlet with a dry brush every six months; do not use compressed air or solvents.
