# Travel and Expense Policy (2026 edition)

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective date: 1 March 2026
This edition supersedes the 2024 edition. Where the two editions differ, this edition applies to all trips that start on or after 1 March 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Tarnwick Systems employees who travel on company business, including interns. Contractors follow the terms of their statement of work.

## 2. Pre-trip approval

Trips with an estimated total cost above EUR 2,000 require approval from a department director in the travel tool before booking. All other trips need line manager approval only.

## 3. Transport

Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class may be booked for flights longer than eight hours with director approval. For journeys under 500 km where a direct train exists, rail travel is expected instead of flying.

Employees who use a private car for business travel are reimbursed at EUR 0.34 per kilometre, up to a maximum of 400 km per trip. Mileage is calculated from the employee's normal place of work.

## 4. Meals and daily allowance

The daily meal allowance is EUR 50 for domestic trips and EUR 70 for international trips. It is paid per calendar day away from the home office and does not require meal receipts.

## 5. Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for every expense above EUR 40. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete a Missing Receipt Declaration (form FIN-07) within 10 working days of the expense. A maximum of three missing receipt declarations are accepted per calendar year.

## 6. Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the end of the trip. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.
