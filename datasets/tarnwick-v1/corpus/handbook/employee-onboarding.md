# Employee Onboarding Guide

Document owner: People Operations
Applies to: permanent and fixed-term employees

## Before the first day

The new employee's laptop is shipped to their home address five working days before the start date. An onboarding buddy from the same department is assigned by the hiring manager before the first day.

## First week

On the first day, the employee meets their manager and buddy and receives access to email, chat and the HR portal. Production system access is never granted in the first week.

## Mandatory training

The information security awareness course must be completed within 14 days of the start date. The data protection course must be completed within 30 days of the start date. Access to customer data is only granted after both courses are completed.

## Probation

The probation period for permanent employees is six months. A probation review meeting with the manager takes place in the fifth month.

## Equipment and accounts

Every employee receives a hardware security key in the first week. The key is required to sign in to all internal systems from the second week onwards.
