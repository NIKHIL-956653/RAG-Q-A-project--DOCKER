# Learning and Development Budget

Document owner: People Operations

## Annual budget

Every permanent employee has a personal learning budget of EUR 1,000 per calendar year. The budget can be used for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Approval

Purchases up to EUR 300 can be made without approval and claimed through the finance portal. Larger purchases need approval from the line manager before booking. Conference travel follows the travel and expense policy and is not paid from the learning budget.

## Study time

Employees may spend up to five working days per year on learning activities during working hours, agreed in advance with their manager.

## Certifications

If the company pays for a certification exam and the employee leaves within 12 months of passing it, the exam fee must be repaid in full. Failed exam attempts are paid once; repeated attempts are paid by the employee.
