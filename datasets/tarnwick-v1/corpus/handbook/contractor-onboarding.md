# Contractor Onboarding Guide

Document owner: Procurement and IT Service Desk
Applies to: external contractors and agency staff working on a statement of work

## Devices and workspace

Contractors do not receive a company laptop. They work through a virtual desktop account, which is activated within two working days after the statement of work is signed. Personal devices may be used to reach the virtual desktop, but company data must never be stored on them.

## Accounts and access

Every contractor has a named Tarnwick sponsor, usually the project manager. Contractor accounts expire automatically on the end date of the statement of work. An extension must be requested by the sponsor at least five working days before the expiry date.

## Mandatory training

Contractors must complete the information security awareness course within seven days of their account being activated. Contractors do not take the employee data protection course; instead they sign the contractor confidentiality agreement before activation.

## Working hours

Contractors agree their working hours with their sponsor. The core collaboration hours that apply to employees do not apply to contractors.
