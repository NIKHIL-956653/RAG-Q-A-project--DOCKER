# Remote Work Policy

Document owner: People Operations
Last reviewed: 12 January 2026

## Eligibility

All permanent employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to four days per week. Roles that require on-site lab access, such as hardware test engineers, are agreed individually with the department director.

## Core collaboration hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time on working days. Meetings involving more than one team should be scheduled inside these hours.

## Home office equipment

Each eligible employee receives a one-time home office stipend of EUR 600 for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment. Receipts for the stipend must be uploaded to the finance portal within 90 days of purchase. A monthly internet allowance of EUR 25 is paid with the salary.

Company laptops, headsets and security keys remain company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## Working from another country

Employees may work from another EU or EEA country for up to 30 working days per calendar year. The request must be approved by People Operations at least 14 days before the first day abroad. Working from a country outside the EU and EEA is not permitted, because of tax and data protection obligations.

## Security requirements

Remote work must take place on a private network or over the company VPN. Working in public spaces is allowed only with a privacy screen filter, and confidential calls must not be taken in public places.
