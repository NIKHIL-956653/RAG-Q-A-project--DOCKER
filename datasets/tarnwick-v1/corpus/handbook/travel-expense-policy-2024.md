# Travel and Expense Policy (2024 edition)

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective date: 1 January 2024
Status: Superseded by the 2026 edition on 1 March 2026. This edition is retained for audit reference only and must not be used for new claims.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Tarnwick Systems employees who travel on company business. Contractors follow the terms of their statement of work and are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Pre-trip approval

Trips with an estimated total cost above EUR 1,500 require written approval from a department director before any booking is made. Trips below that amount only need approval from the employee's line manager.

## 3. Transport

Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class may be booked for flights longer than eight hours with director approval.

Employees who use a private car for business travel are reimbursed at EUR 0.30 per kilometre. Mileage is calculated from the employee's normal place of work, not from home.

## 4. Meals and daily allowance

A daily meal allowance of EUR 45 applies to domestic trips and EUR 60 to international trips. The allowance is paid per calendar day away from the home office and does not require meal receipts.

## 5. Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for every expense above EUR 25. Card statements are not accepted as a substitute for an itemised receipt.

## 6. Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 60 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later are reviewed case by case and may be rejected.
