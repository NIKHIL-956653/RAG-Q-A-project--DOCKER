# Access Review Procedure

Document owner: Security and Compliance
Version: 1.4

## Quarterly reviews

Access to production systems is reviewed every quarter. Each system owner confirms or removes every user's access within 10 working days after the end of the quarter. Access that has not been confirmed by the deadline is removed automatically.

## Privileged access

Administrator and other privileged accounts must sign in with a FIDO2 hardware security key. Shared administrator accounts are not allowed. Privileged access is reviewed monthly rather than quarterly.

## Leavers

When People Operations records a termination, all access must be revoked within 4 hours. For contractors, the sponsor is responsible for confirming that access was revoked at the end of the statement of work.

## Evidence

Review results are stored in the compliance tool for 400 days, so they are available to external auditors.
