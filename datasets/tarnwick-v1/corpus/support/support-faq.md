# Customer Support FAQ

Document owner: Customer Support
Last updated: 20 January 2026

## When is support available?

Customers on the Standard plan can reach support Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, excluding public holidays. Enterprise plan customers have 24/7 support for priority 1 issues.

## How fast will support respond?

Standard plan tickets receive a first response within one business day. For Enterprise customers, priority 1 tickets receive a first response within 1 hour and priority 2 tickets within 4 hours.

## What warranty do Tarnwick devices have?

The KX-200 sensor has a 2-year limited warranty. The KX-220 sensor has a 3-year limited warranty. The Relay Gateway has a 2-year limited warranty. Batteries are consumables and are not covered by the warranty.

## How do I return a faulty device?

Open a return request (RMA) in the customer portal. A replacement unit is shipped within five working days after the faulty unit arrives at our repair centre. Devices damaged by incorrect installation are not replaced free of charge.

## Can I move a sensor to another gateway?

Yes. Remove the sensor from its current gateway in the customer portal first; a sensor can be registered to only one gateway at a time.
